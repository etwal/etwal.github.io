CNN for License Plate Identification

CNNPythonDataset Curation

For this project, I used a Convolution Neural Network to Identify the characters in an image of a License plate.

I sorted the images of characters into folders and I gave each character a one-hot encoder vector so when the prediction vector is output I can figure out which letter the network thinks is in the photo and how strong the prediction was. These vectors also helped as they quantified the characters.

I created a data set of 540 characters and I split the dataset into 60% training 20% validation 20% testing.
