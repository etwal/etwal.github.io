I owned the controls subsystem, replacing an oscillating PD tracker with an adaptive Pure Pursuit controller, a turn in place recovery state machine, and Menger curvature speed profiling. Plasma Donut Controller Capstone

My first Engineering Physics capstone: a device that creates and confines a radio frequency plasma toroid inside a globe of noble gas. I owned the driver circuit, a Class E oscillator that puts a large 13 MHz current through the primary coil, and modelled the plasma in LTspice as a loosely coupled secondary. The toroid only appeared once I got the MOSFETs zero voltage switching, which moved the losses out of the transistors and into the gas.
